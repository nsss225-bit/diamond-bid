Diamond Bid

A card game of secret bids for 2 or 4 players.

How to play

Thirteen cards are turned up in the middle, one at a time. Everyone bids one card from their own suit face down, all bids flip together, and the highest card takes the middle card's points (A = 1 up to K = 13). Cards you bid are gone, so plan when to spend your Kings. After 13 rounds, the most points wins. A tie in a round shares the points, or cancels out if you choose that rule.

New to the game? Open Play → Tutorial in the app for a two-minute guided game.

Questions and problems

Report a problem or ask a question on the Diamond Bid help page. Please include your device model, the app version (shown at the bottom of the How to play tab) and what happened.

Your progress

Coins, characters, card designs and achievements are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. There are no purchases with real money.
