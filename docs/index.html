Diamond Bid

A card game of secret bids for 2 or 4 players.

Play now, free

Open Diamond Bid to play in your browser. To keep it on your phone like an app:

  • iPhone and iPad: open the link in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

It opens full screen from its own icon and works without internet once it has been opened once.

How to play

Thirteen cards are turned up in the middle, one at a time. Everyone bids one card from their own suit face down, all bids flip together, and the highest card takes the middle card's points (A = 1 up to K = 13). Cards you bid are gone, so plan when to spend your Kings. After 13 rounds, the most points wins. A tie in a round splits the points between the tied players, and a tied match is settled by the tie-breaker wheel.

New to the game? Open Play → Tutorial in the app for a two-minute guided game.
