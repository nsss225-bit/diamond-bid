Privacy policy

Diamond Bid for iPhone, iPad and Android · Last updated 17 October 2026

The short version

Diamond Bid does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your game progress (coins, characters and card designs you unlocked, achievements, your best daily-challenge scores and your settings such as language, sound and vibration) and the optional player name you type are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network use

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts when you are online. Google receives the standard technical information any web request includes (such as your IP address) and handles it under its own privacy policy. The game works without a connection.

Children

Diamond Bid is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Purchases

There are no purchases with real money. Coins are earned only by playing.

Changes

If a future version adds features that need personal information (for example online play or signing in), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's privacy details on the App Store and Google Play will change to match.

Contact

Questions about this policy: Diamond Bid help page.
