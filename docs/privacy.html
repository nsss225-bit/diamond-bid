Privacy policy

Diamond Bid for iPhone, iPad and Android · Last updated 19 October 2026

The short version

Diamond Bid has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. When you play online or post a daily-challenge score, the game sends only what other players need to see: the player name you chose, your character, and your moves or score. It is never sold or used for anything else.

What the app stores

Your game progress (coins, characters and card designs you unlocked, achievements, your best daily-challenge scores and your settings such as language, sound and vibration) is saved only on your own device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Online play and the daily leaderboard

Online play and the daily leaderboard run on Google Firebase, a server service from Google. The app gets a random anonymous player ID from Firebase. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple or Google account.

  • Online tables and quick match: while you are at a table, the other players there see your player name, your character and your moves. This is removed from the server as soon as you leave the table or close the app.
  • Daily leaderboard: when you finish the daily challenge, your best score for the day, your player name and your character are posted so other players can see them. Each new day's score replaces the last one.

Your player name is whatever you type, or your character's name if you leave it empty, so please don't use your real full name if you'd rather not. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms, and like any server it receives standard technical information such as your IP address. When the app opens with an internet connection, it gets its anonymous ID and loads today's leaderboard; apart from that, nothing is sent unless you play online or finish the daily challenge.

To have your leaderboard entry removed, contact us on the help page below.

Network use

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts when you are online. Google receives the standard technical information any web request includes (such as your IP address) and handles it under its own privacy policy. The game works without a connection.

Children

Diamond Bid is suitable for all ages. It asks no one, including children, for personal information. The only thing other players see is the player name and character chosen in the game.

Purchases

There are no purchases with real money. Coins are earned only by playing.

Changes

If a future version changes what is shared (for example signing in), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's privacy details on the App Store and Google Play will change to match.
